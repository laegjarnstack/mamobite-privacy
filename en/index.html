MamoBite Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2025

Türkçe

1. Overview

MamoBite is a mobile app that helps parents discover baby food recipes. This privacy policy explains what data is processed when you use the app and how it is protected.

MamoBite does not require you to create an account or sign in. Most of your information stays on your device only.

2. Data controller

Data controller: MamoBite

Contact: laegjarnq@gmail.com

3. Data stored on your device

The following information is kept only on your device (local storage) and is not uploaded to our servers:

• Baby age, allergen, and dietary preferences

• Language selection (Turkish / English)

• Favorite recipes and recipe interactions

• Daily recommendation history

• AI session data (anonymous session token and daily usage quota)

You can reset this data from Settings or delete it by uninstalling the app.

4. Data processed on our servers

When you use AI-powered recipe suggestions, requests are handled through an Edge Function hosted on Supabase. In this process:

• A device-specific anonymous install secret is one-way hashed for quota tracking; the raw value is not stored.

• Recipe ranking requests (e.g. available ingredients, reference recipe, locale) are sent to our AI provider.

• Request and response content is not persistently stored.

• Operational logs contain only hashed session/IP prefixes, operation type, latency, and token counts.

5. Third-party services

• Supabase: application infrastructure and AI API hosting.

• Google Gemini: AI-powered recipe ranking (in production). Requests are subject to Google’s privacy policy.

MamoBite does not use third-party analytics or behavioral tracking for advertising.

6. Children’s privacy

MamoBite is designed for parents and caregivers. The app does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Baby age and allergen information is entered by a parent or caregiver for recipe filtering and is stored on the device.

7. Purpose of processing

Data is processed only to personalize recipe suggestions, apply allergen and age filters, manage AI quotas, and protect the service from abuse.

Your data is not sold or used for advertising profiling.

8. Retention

On-device data remains until you reset it or uninstall the app. Server-side anonymous session tokens are valid for approximately 24 hours. Operational logs are retained according to our infrastructure provider’s standard retention periods.

9. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. On-device data can be reset from Settings. For server-side data, contact laegjarnq@gmail.com.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date on this page. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
